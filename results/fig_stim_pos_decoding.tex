\caption{\textbf{On-screen stimulus azimuth is recoverable from wheel position and trial timing.} Ground truth is the Bonsai stimulus-position log (\texttt{\_iblrig\_stimPositionScreen}), aligned to photodiode stimOn (residual SD 9 ms). \textbf{a--c}, Example trials from session \texttt{6713a4a7}, showing stimulus azimuth against time from stimOn. Lines: logged truth (thick grey); physics model $x_0 + G\,\Delta w(t-\tau)$, $|G|=124^\circ$/rad, with the side known (dashed black); the same model with the side inferred from the wheel direction at response and the outcome-specific stimOff delay (blue); a gradient-boosting model (GBM) given only wheel displacement and time since stimOn (T0, red). The vertical line marks the response time. \textbf{a}, Correct trial with the stimulus starting on the left ($-35^\circ$) and brought to the centre. \textbf{b}, Correct trial with the stimulus starting on the right ($+35^\circ$). \textbf{c}, Error trial: the stimulus is driven away from the centre, to $\approx70^\circ$. The physics models overlie the truth. The T0 model cannot know the starting side, so it wanders near the average of the two possibilities. \textbf{d--f}, Mean absolute error per session (dots, $n=8$ sessions, one per lab; bars, median across sessions; log scale) for each model: physics with side known or inferred, and GBM with features T2 (wheel + all event times), T1 (wheel + response time) or T0 (wheel only), cross-validated in 5 folds grouped by trial. The dotted line marks one encoder step ($0.76^\circ$). \textbf{d}, Samples from stimOn to response on answered trials. \textbf{e}, Samples from response to stimOff on answered trials; the error of $\approx2^\circ$ comes from Bonsai freezing on the last frame before the threshold event. \textbf{f}, Samples from no-go trials (only sessions with no-go trials). Without the side, no decoder can place the stimulus. \textbf{g}, Display lag $\tau$ fitted per session against the magnitude of an unconstrained least-squares gain (dotted line: nominal 124$^\circ$/rad).}
